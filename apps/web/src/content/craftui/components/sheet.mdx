## Anatomy

#### Usage

```tsx
<SheetRoot>
  <SheetTrigger />
  <SheetContent side="right">
    <SheetHeader>
      <SheetTitle />
      <SheetDescription />
    </SheetHeader>
    <SheetPanel />
    <SheetFooter>
      <SheetClose />
    </SheetFooter>
  </SheetContent>
</SheetRoot>
```

```tsx
<Sheet.Root>
  <Sheet.Trigger />
  <Sheet.Content side="right">
    <Sheet.Header>
      <Sheet.Title />
      <Sheet.Description />
    </Sheet.Header>
    <Sheet.Panel />
    <Sheet.Footer>
      <Sheet.Close />
    </Sheet.Footer>
  </Sheet.Content>
</Sheet.Root>
```
