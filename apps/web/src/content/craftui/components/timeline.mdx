## Anatomy

#### Usage

```tsx
<TimelineRoot>
  <TimelineItem>
    <TimelineMarker />
    <TimelineConnector />
    <TimelineContent>
      <TimelineTime />
      <TimelineTitle />
      <TimelineDescription />
    </TimelineContent>
  </TimelineItem>
</TimelineRoot>
```

```tsx
<Timeline.Root>
  <Timeline.Item>
    <Timeline.Marker />
    <Timeline.Connector />
    <Timeline.Content>
      <Timeline.Time />
      <Timeline.Title />
      <Timeline.Description />
    </Timeline.Content>
  </Timeline.Item>
</Timeline.Root>
```
