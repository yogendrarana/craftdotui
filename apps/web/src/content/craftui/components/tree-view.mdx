## Anatomy

```tsx
<TreeRoot value={selected} onValueChange={setSelected}>
  <TreeItem value="node-1" label="Node 1">
    <TreeItemTrigger>
      <TreeItemChevron />
      <TreeItemIcon />
      <TreeItemLabel>Node 1</TreeItemLabel>
    </TreeItemTrigger>
    <TreeItemContent>
      <TreeItem value="node-1-1" label="Subnode 1">
        <TreeItemTrigger>
          <TreeItemLabel>Subnode 1</TreeItemLabel>
        </TreeItemTrigger>
      </TreeItem>
    </TreeItemContent>
  </TreeItem>
</TreeRoot>
```
