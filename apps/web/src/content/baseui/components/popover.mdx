## Anatomy

#### Components

```tsx
<PopoverRoot>
  <PopoverTrigger />
  <PopoverContent>
    <PopoverTitle />
    <PopoverDescription />
    <PopoverClose />
  </PopoverContent>
</PopoverRoot>
```

#### Primitives (Base UI)

```tsx
<Popover.Root>
  <Popover.Trigger />
  <Popover.Portal>
    <Popover.Backdrop />
    <Popover.Positioner>
      <Popover.Popup>
        <Popover.Arrow />
        <Popover.Viewport>
          <Popover.Title />
          <Popover.Description />
          <Popover.Close />
        </Popover.Viewport>
      </Popover.Popup>
    </Popover.Positioner>
  </Popover.Portal>
</Popover.Root>
```
